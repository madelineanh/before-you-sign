      How it works

      Three independent reviews, one clear report.

      Ownership

      We identify the owner of record, the size of their Connecticut holdings, and the people registered behind any LLC.

      Property & area

      We review the building's assessor record, service complaints at and near the address, local eviction filings, and flood risk.

      Lease compliance

      Each clause is checked against Connecticut tenant law, from deposit limits and late fees to entry rights and prohibited waivers.

      Cited, not guessedEvery finding links to the public record or statute it comes from.
      Private by designYour lease is read once for your review; the full text is never saved.
      Not a people searchWe review properties and companies, not private individuals.
